# Julie la Compétitrice — The Active Member

> Secondary persona: The power user whose daily engagement proves the platform works

**Document:** Trigger Map - Secondary Persona
**Created:** 2026-03-22
**Status:** COMPLETE
**Priority:** 🚀 SECONDARY TARGET

## Who Julie Is

Julie is 32, a competitive canicross and canitrail runner. She trains 3-4 times a week with her border collie, competes in 10-15 races per season, and is active in her club's social life. She's in the app daily — checking upcoming events, coordinating carpools, sharing race results. She's tech-comfortable, uses her phone for everything, and expects modern UX. She's the member who makes the club feel alive online.

---

## Psychological Profile

Julie values **community and performance** in equal measure. Running with her dog is deeply personal — the human-animal bond is her primary motivation. But the competitive and social dimensions amplify everything: she tracks her progress, compares times, coordinates with clubmates, and plans her season around the race calendar.

She's a **natural networker** within the club. She's the one who creates the WhatsApp group for carpools, posts race photos on Facebook, and nudges other members to sign up for events. This makes her CaniFed's potential champion — if the platform works, she'll pull others in. If it doesn't, she'll be the loudest critic.

Julie has **zero patience for broken tools**. She's used CaniClub Connect and experienced the frustration firsthand: GPS coordinates that send you to the wrong field, chat messages that never arrive, an event list that's confusing to navigate. She's adapted by building workarounds on Facebook and WhatsApp, which work but fragment her club experience across four different apps.

She's **mobile-first by nature**. She checks her phone at the trailhead, in the car before a race, during lunch breaks. If the app isn't fast, intuitive, and beautiful on her phone, she won't use it. She expects the same UX quality she gets from consumer apps — Strava, Instagram, Waze.

---

## Internal State

When Julie thinks about club digital tools, she feels **impatient frustration mixed with resigned acceptance**. She knows CaniClub Connect is terrible but everyone uses it because there's nothing else. She's compensating with Facebook groups and WhatsApp, which means her club experience is scattered across four platforms. She'd love one place where everything lives — but she's skeptical that one exists.

When she discovers a new tool that might replace the patchwork, she feels **cautious excitement**. She'll test it immediately — can I see my club's events? Does the map work? Can I share a carpool thread? If the first experience is smooth, her excitement builds quickly. She's an early adopter by temperament.

---

## Usage Context

**Access/Discovery:** Julie finds CaniFed because her club admin (Perrine) introduces it. Or she sees another club using it at a competition and asks about it. She downloads immediately.

**Emotional State:** Energetic and evaluative. She's testing: is this better than what I have? She'll compare directly to the Facebook/WhatsApp/CaniClub Connect combo she currently uses. The bar isn't perfection — it's "noticeably better than the patchwork."

**Behavior Pattern:** Julie navigates quickly. She opens the app, checks upcoming events, taps one to see details and who's going, checks the GPS meeting point, and either joins or moves on. She uses search, filters by date, and expects real-time updates. She interacts with chat/comments to coordinate logistics.

**Decision Criteria:** (1) Events are easy to find and join. (2) GPS works perfectly every time. (3) Other club members are actually on the platform. (4) The app feels modern and fast.

**Success Outcome:** Julie opens CaniFed, sees the weekend's canitrail, checks that her friend Sophie is going, joins the carpool thread, and taps the GPS pin for Waze directions on Saturday morning — all without touching Facebook or WhatsApp. She deletes the old WhatsApp group.

## Driving Forces

### Wants (Positive Drivers)

**✅ See upcoming events, who's going, and join instantly**
- WHAT: Event list with clear details, participant list, and one-tap registration
- WHY: Planning her week and season depends on knowing what's happening and who's joining
- WHEN: Daily check-ins, especially Monday (planning the week) and Thursday (confirming the weekend)
- **CaniFed Promise:** Clean event feed with date, location, map preview, and "going" list. One tap to join. Filter by type (training, competition, rando). See your clubmates.

**✅ Coordinate carpools and logistics in one place**
- WHAT: Event-scoped conversations where members organize transport, gear, and meeting times
- WHY: Currently spread across 3-4 platforms; she loses track of who offered to drive
- WHEN: 48-72 hours before an event, the coordination window opens
- **CaniFed Promise:** Event-attached chat where carpool offers, departure times, and logistics live next to the event itself. No more searching through WhatsApp threads.

**✅ Tap GPS and navigate to the meeting point on race day**
- WHAT: Working map with accurate GPS pin, one-tap redirect to Waze or Google Maps
- WHY: Races and training sessions happen in forests, fields, and trail systems — finding the exact meeting point is non-trivial
- WHEN: Race morning, often driving to an unfamiliar location with her dog in the back seat
- **CaniFed Promise:** Event detail page with map, accurate GPS coordinates, and "Y aller avec Waze" button. One tap from event to navigation. Zero ambiguity.

### Fears (Negative Drivers)

**❌ Broken maps send her to the wrong field again**
- WHAT: GPS coordinates that are inaccurate, maps that don't render, or navigation that opens the wrong location
- WHY: She's experienced this with CaniClub Connect multiple times — arriving late to a race because the pin was 2km off
- WHEN: Race day, when stress is already high and every minute matters
- **CaniFed Answer:** GPS accuracy is a non-negotiable technical requirement. Map pins are verified, coordinates are displayed for manual check, and the Waze/Google Maps handoff is tested on every device.

**❌ Important information buried in Facebook group noise**
- WHAT: Critical event updates (time change, cancellation, route modification) lost in a stream of memes, photos, and off-topic chat
- WHY: Facebook groups have no structure; a crucial update posted at 7am is buried by noon
- WHEN: Before events, when last-minute changes happen and she can't find the update
- **CaniFed Answer:** Event updates are attached to the event itself, not buried in a feed. Push notifications for changes to events you've joined. The information lives where you expect it.

**❌ Another app that nobody else in the club uses**
- WHAT: She adopts the new tool but her clubmates stay on Facebook/WhatsApp, leaving her in a ghost town
- WHY: The value of a club platform is proportional to how many club members are on it — network effects
- WHEN: First weeks after the club launches on CaniFed, the critical mass window
- **CaniFed Answer:** The admin (Perrine) drives adoption for the whole club. Event notifications pull members in. The platform only needs to be "where the events are" to reach critical mass — members follow the events.

## Relationship to Business Goals

- ⭐ **Club Adoption (THE ENGINE):** Julie validates the platform through daily use. Her enthusiasm (or complaints) influence the admin's decision to keep or abandon CaniFed.
- 🚀 **Member Engagement:** She IS the engagement metric. 80%+ WAU depends on users like Julie checking the app daily. Her satisfaction predicts retention.
- 🌟 **Club Autonomy:** Julie benefits from in-app coordination replacing scattered workarounds. One platform = one club life = less friction.
