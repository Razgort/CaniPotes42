# 01: Perrine's Club Setup

**Project:** CaniFed  
**Created:** 2026-03-22  
**Method:** Whiteport Design Studio (WDS)

---

## Transaction (Q1)

**What this scenario covers:**  
Perrine creates her club’s digital home, finishes essential settings, and sends the first invitations so members can join—without training or support calls.

---

## Business Goal (Q2)

**Goal:** ⭐ Club Adoption — THE ENGINE  
**Objective:** 10 clubs onboarded in 12 months; each admin unlocks 30–150 members; setup must feel effortless on day one.

---

## User & Situation (Q3)

**Persona:** Perrine l'Organisatrice (PRIMARY)  
**Situation:** After a peer recommendation at a regional FFSLC event, she opens CaniFed the same evening on her phone to “finally stop juggling Facebook and Excel” before the next training block.

---

## Driving Forces (Q4)

**Hope:** She wants a working club space in under ten minutes with zero manual reading.  

**Worry:** She fears members will ignore the tool and drift back to WhatsApp and Facebook.

---

## Device & Starting Point (Q5 + Q6)

**Device:** Mobile (primary); may finish invites on laptop later.  
**Entry:** She opens the link shared by another club president (`canifed` landing) from her messages app and scrolls to confirm it is credible before signing up.

---

## Best Outcome (Q7)

**User Success:** Club exists with name, federation context, visual identity, first co-admin role set, and invitation emails queued—she feels “we’re live.”  

**Business Success:** One qualified club onboarded with admin confident enough to invite real members; measurable funnel toward the 10-club goal.

---

## Shortest Path (Q8)

1. **Landing Page** — Confirms value for canine sports clubs, trusts positioning, taps primary CTA to register.  
2. **Sign Up** — Creates account with email + password, accepts privacy notice.  
3. **Club Creation Flow** — Enters club name, federation (optional), description, uploads logo.  
4. **Club Settings** — Reviews and saves club details and defaults she needs before inviting people.  
5. **Member Invite Flow** — Pastes or enters emails and sends invitations to core members.  
6. **Role Management** — Promotes a trusted co-organizer to admin so she is not alone. ✓

---
